Root cause
----------

TileStateCtrl sized exeTileEnReq and exeRelatedTileReg with numIOB rather than
numTiles.  The production CSTORE generation has six IOB banks and eight tiles,
so normal STORE's 0x94 dependency mask was truncated to 0x14.  Tile 7 was
omitted, the target IOB execution enable fell nine cycles before its routed
0x4567 data arrived, and SRAM received one zero-data write.

Production fix
--------------

In cgra-mg/src/main/scala/vitra/VitraCGRAController.scala:

- exeTileEnReq is now UInt(numTiles.W).
- exeRelatedTileReg is now UInt(numTiles.W).

exeIobEnReq and exeIobEnReg remain UInt(numIOB.W).  No CSTORE operand,
UseEn/UseAddr/IsStore, Mux, DelayPipe, CLOAD, or physical-operation contract
changed.

Regression
----------

cgra-mg/src/test/scala/vitra/VitraTileStateCtrlSpec.scala instantiates the real
TileStateCtrl with numIOB=6 and numTiles=8.  It proves that the tile request and
completion masks are eight bits wide, that completion of tiles 0..6 does not
end execution while tile 7 is outstanding, and that execution completes only
